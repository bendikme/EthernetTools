## EthernetTools 26.11.6

### New: where This PC is, from spanning tree and CDP

- The scan and the Topology window now also read the **spanning-tree** packets (BPDUs) and Cisco's **CDP**
  that the switch sends on this PC's port. Both name the switch and the port this PC is plugged into, even when
  the network card keeps LLDP to itself (some Intel X710 cards). Spanning tree also names the **root bridge**. A
  switch that is one link's cost from the root is drawn as probably cabled straight to it (a dotted line).

### New: switches and access points that only speak SNMPv3

- Every device that stays silent on SNMP v1/v2c is asked whether it speaks SNMPv3. The maker in its answer (or
  its MAC prefix) tells switches and access points apart from printers and servers. Those made by a network
  maker are drawn as **SWITCH?** boxes and named in the orange warning: a v3 user would let the map read them.

### New: duplicate addresses and names

- After a scan, an IP address that two or more devices claim (or a router answering for others), and a name of
  station on two or more Profinet devices, show in a new **Problem** column, with the row in red and a count in
  the status bar.

### New: Profinet inventory

- **Read I&M** reads I&M0 from every Profinet device with an IP address: order number, serial number, hardware
  and firmware revision, in their own columns.

### New: save, compare and merge maps; draw.io

- The Topology window's **Map** menu:
  - **Save map...** saves the map to a file.
  - **Compare with a saved map...** lists what is new, gone or moved since then, and badges the boxes on the map.
  - **Add a map saved on another PC...** adds the ports that PC knew, its own above all, so a few PCs at
    different desks fill in ports no switch tells.
  - **Export draw.io...** saves the map as a draw.io diagram (and from there for Visio), next to **Export PNG...**.
- The CSV export has the Problem and I&M columns.
