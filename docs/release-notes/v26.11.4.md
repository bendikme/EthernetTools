## EthernetTools 26.11.4

### Fixed

- **Topology: devices that fail GETBULK are now read.** Some small SNMP agents, in Profinet devices and small
  switches, answer EthernetTools' fast table read (GETBULK) with an error, or not at all. Before, the map then got
  no LLDP neighbours or MAC table from that device and lost its links. Now EthernetTools asks again with fewer
  rows per request, then one row at a time (GETNEXT), from where it stopped.

### Notes

- `EthernetTools.exe --selftest` now also checks these fallbacks against a small SNMP agent of its own on
  127.0.0.1.
