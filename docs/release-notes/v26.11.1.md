## EthernetTools 26.11.1

### New

- **Set Name** gives the selected device its Profinet *name of station* (DCP Set NameOfStation, kept after a
  power cycle). It is on the toolbar, in the right-click menu and on F2. The dialog checks the name as you type it,
  with the Profinet rules ProfinetTools uses:
  - small letters a–z, digits, hyphens and dots;
  - at most 240 characters, and at most 63 between dots;
  - not an IP address, and not `port-xyz`.

  Capitals turn into small letters as you type. If another device in the list already has the name, it asks
  first. Then it waits for the device's answer and says what the device answered: done, or why not (for
  example, a device connected to its controller refuses a new name).

### Fixed

- **Flash LED** now waits for the device's answer and says what it was. Before, it reported "the LED should
  blink" even when no device answered or the device refused.
- **Flash LED during a scan** no longer stops the scan. Before, the scan stopped finding devices after the
  flash and didn't say so.
- **Tagged Profinet answers:** the scan now also reads Profinet answers sent with an 802.1Q (priority) tag.
  Before, it missed those devices.

### Notes

- `EthernetTools.exe --selftest` checks the Profinet frames, the name rules and the Set Name dialog, without
  a network. The release build runs it.
- Npcap is still needed for scanning, Flash LED and Set Name (<https://npcap.com>).
