## EthernetTools 26.11.3

### Improved: Topology map

- **Switches the scan didn't find are asked too.** The Topology window asks the scanned devices over SNMP,
  then asks the management address each LLDP neighbour announces. It keeps going until no new address turns
  up, as PRONETA and TIA Portal do. A switch the scan missed, or one on a management subnet of its own, now
  shows its ports and MAC table if it answers SNMP.
- **The window says why the map is incomplete.** An orange note in the status bar names the switches that
  didn't answer SNMP. It also warns when this PC heard no LLDP, so the switch port it is plugged into is
  unknown. Hover the note for details: each switch's IP address, the SNMP community used, and what to change.
  Hovering a silent switch on the map says the same.
- No LLDP heard often means the switch port doesn't send it. It can also mean the network card keeps LLDP to
  itself: some Intel X710, XL710 and E810 cards have a firmware LLDP agent that does.

### Fixed

- A switch that answers SNMP on two of its addresses counts once. Before, it counted twice. That could make a
  single device on a port look like an "Unmanaged switch?".

### Notes

- The map can only show a switch's ports and MAC table if the switch answers SNMP. Enable SNMP v1/v2c
  read-only on your switches, for the PC that draws the map, to get the whole map.
